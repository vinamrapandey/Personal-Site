**Transpent** is an offline-first ledger app for small, often women-led shops
that have no reliable way to track customer credit and supplier bills without
internet access.

Everything is stored locally through Room/SQLite so the app works fully offline,
with a clean MVVM architecture and CSV export for when records need to leave the
device. It's built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and distributed through GitHub
releases, under active development.
