**HireUltra** is an AI recruitment agent that sources and scores candidates
across LinkedIn and the open web — solving the problem that manual resume
screening doesn't scale past a handful of roles.

It uses a tiered sourcing strategy (free DuckDuckGo search → Serper X-Ray search
→ LinkedIn) to keep cost near zero until precision is actually needed, parses
job descriptions, and scores each candidate against the role with visual match
tags — reaching 87% match accuracy in testing.
